Revision MDT Simulator

Several AI "consultants" each give an opinion on a case, read each other's views and argue it out, then a chair records a final decision. Every card is one call to the AI provider using your own API key.

Fictional or fully anonymised cases only. Text you run here is sent to the AI provider you choose. Do not enter identifiable patient information unless your trust's information governance has approved it. Output is not clinical advice.

Who sits at the table

The meeting hasn't started.
  1. First opinions. Each consultant reads the case on their own and gives a view, without seeing anyone else's.
  2. Discussion. Everyone reads all the other opinions and says where they agree, disagree, or change their mind.
  3. Chair's decision. The chair reads the whole discussion and writes the outcome, recording any dissent.
Paste your API key, pick a case and press Hold the meeting. A full run makes about 9 to 14 calls and takes a minute or two.
This page has no server. It runs entirely in your browser and talks directly to the provider you choose. Your key and your cases are not sent to whoever hosts this page. Usage is billed to your own provider account, so it's sensible to set a monthly spending limit there.
