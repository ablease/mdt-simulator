Revision MDT Simulator

Several AI "consultants" each give an opinion on a case, read each other's views and argue it out, then a chair records a final decision. Every card is one call to an AI provider using your own API key, and each seat at the table can use a different model.

Fictional or fully anonymised cases only. Text you run here is sent to the AI providers you choose. Do not enter identifiable patient information unless your trust's information governance has approved it. Output is not clinical advice.

Your API keys

Add a key for one provider, or both if you want consultants on different providers.

Model not in the list?

Type the exact model name from your provider's documentation and add it.

The case

Change any detail (say, the aspirate result) and rerun to see whether the panel changes its mind. Choose "Blank case" to write your own.

Who sits at the table

The meeting hasn't started.
  1. First opinions. Each consultant reads the case on their own and gives a view, without seeing anyone else's.
  2. Discussion. Everyone reads all the other opinions and says where they agree, disagree, or change their mind.
  3. Chair's decision. The chair reads the whole discussion and writes the outcome, recording any dissent.
Add an API key, pick a case and press Hold the meeting. A full run makes about 9 to 14 calls and takes a minute or two. Afterwards you can download the meeting as a PDF report or as JSON data.
This page has no server. It runs entirely in your browser and talks directly to the providers you choose. Your keys and your cases are not sent to whoever hosts this page. Usage is billed to your own provider accounts, so it's sensible to set a monthly spending limit there.
